Assumptions I designed against

Equal trio — mortgages, property, health cover — held together by one "concierge" promise, not three separate businesses. Nav gives each equal weight; the homepage sells the bundle.

Audience is buying from 6,000km away: every screen answers "can I even do this as a non-resident?" before it sells. USD/CAD figures shown alongside €, and a Spain-vs-home-country comparison block instead of generic FAQ.

Trust is carried by hard numbers and partner-bank logos, per the reference site. Advisor photos are secondary.

Bilingual ES/EN: nav carries a real language switch; Spanish copy runs ~20% longer, so headline boxes are sized with slack.
